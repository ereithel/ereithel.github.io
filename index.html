Background

About Eric

I'm an Emmy Award-winning video editor and broadcast journalist based in the Chicago area, with more than a decade of experience telling stories that audiences actually care about.

My background is in live broadcast news — where speed, accuracy, and editorial instinct aren't optional — but my work spans corporate events, social media content, sizzle reels, and long-form digital production. Whether I'm combing through hours of raw footage to find the moment that makes a highlight reel, or shaping a breaking story for 3.4 million households, the goal is always the same: clarity, pacing, and impact.

I'm also a trained improviser and performer, which means I think fast, adapt quickly, and never mind a creative curveball.
